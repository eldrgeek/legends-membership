HBCU Support

Legends HBCU Scholarship Program

A $10,000 scholarship and career-development track for undergraduates at Historically Black Colleges and Universities, honoring the rich history of HBCUs and their alumni.

← Back to Scholarships & Grants

About the Program

Created through the Legends Care program, this scholarship supports undergraduates attending Historically Black Colleges and Universities, honoring the rich history of HBCUs and their alumni while moving that legacy forward. Applicants do not need to have played basketball.

Award

$10,000 scholarship, plus job-board access, internship placement, complimentary NBA All-Star Weekend attendance with travel and lodging, senior-year career development sessions, and entry into the Legends Scholars Mentoring Program upon graduation.

Contact

HBCU@legendsofbasketball.com or 312-913-9400

Eligibility

  • Rising full-time sophomore, junior, or senior at a four-year HBCU
  • U.S. citizen or permanent resident
  • Minimum 3.7 GPA
  • Annual household income not exceeding $100,000
  • Not eligible if an immediate family member of a current or former NBA, WNBA, ABA, or Harlem Globetrotters player, or of Legends of Basketball staff/board members

How to Apply

Applicants submit their most recent tax return or FAFSA report, one recommendation letter from a faculty member, administrator, or coach, and an official or unofficial transcript.

The 2024–2025 application cycle closed May 15, 2024; the next cycle's dates are announced on the program page each year.

Source: legendsofbasketball.com/hbcu. Visit the canonical page for the most current details and application dates.

Earl Lloyd Scholarship Fund

Funded by the NBA Players Legacy Fund, honoring Earl Lloyd — the first African American to play in the NBA — by supporting the children of Legends of Basketball members.

← Back to Scholarships & Grants

About the Fund

The Earl Lloyd Scholarship Fund provides financial support to children of Legends of Basketball members, funded through an annual gift from the NBA Players Legacy Fund directed toward low-income recipients.

Honoring Earl Lloyd

Earl Francis Lloyd broke the NBA color barrier on October 31, 1950, playing against the Rochester Royals. Nicknamed "The Big Cat," Lloyd was born April 3, 1928, in Alexandria, Virginia. He played collegiately at West Virginia State College and had a nine-season NBA career (1950–1961) with the Washington Capitols, Syracuse Nationals, and Detroit Pistons.

Eligibility

  • Open to children of Legends of Basketball members
  • The member-parent must have played three full years in the NBA
  • Priority given to recipients of the Dave DeBusschere Scholarship
Annual Funding

$45,000 in restricted gifts each year, supporting low-income recipients.

How to Apply

Application procedures are not published; contact the subcommittee leads or the organization directly for the current cycle.

Scholarships & Grants Available to Members

Legends of Basketball administers a set of named scholarship funds and a general member grant program for members and their eligible family members. Each program below has its own eligibility rules, award amount, and application process — read the full details before applying. Content sourced from legendsofbasketball.com/grants-and-scholarships.

  • Dave DeBusschere Scholarship Fund — for members, spouses, and children pursuing higher education
  • Earl Lloyd Scholarship Fund — for children of members, funded by the NBA Players Legacy Fund
  • Legends HBCU Scholarship Program — $10,000 scholarship for HBCU undergraduates
  • Member Grant Program — need-based financial assistance for members in hardship

To apply or learn more — contact Scholarship & Grants subcommittee leads Lionel Hollins (Lihollins14@gmail.com) or George Tinsley (Gtinsley@pengeo.com). For the complete and most current list of all scholarships and grants available through the association, visit legendsofbasketball.com.

Eligibility & Requirements

Scholarship Eligibility

Eligibility is program-specific — each fund above has its own rules for who can apply, award amounts, and how to apply. General Legends of Basketball membership eligibility, referenced by several of the programs, is summarized below. Full detail lives on each program's page; sourced from legendsofbasketball.com/grants-and-scholarships and legendsofbasketball.com/alumnis/membership.

  • NBA, ABA, WNBA, or Harlem Globetrotters alumni are eligible for Legends of Basketball membership
  • Gold ($250/year) and Platinum ($3,500 lifetime) membership tiers; Platinum includes eligibility for the named scholarship funds
  • Health insurance access requires 3+ years of NBA service time
  • Long-tenured annual members receive credits (10%–50% off) toward a Platinum upgrade
  • See each program's page above for its specific income limits, family-relationship rules, application windows, and required documents

Questions about eligibility? Contact subcommittee leads Lionel Hollins (Lihollins14@gmail.com) or George Tinsley (Gtinsley@pengeo.com) for program-specific eligibility details.

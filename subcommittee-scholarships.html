Scholarships & Grants Available to Members

The following scholarships and educational grants are administered by the National Basketball Retired Players Association (NBRPA) — parent organization of Legends of Basketball — for members and their eligible family members. Content sourced from legendsofbasketball.com/grants-and-scholarships.

Named Scholarship
Dave DeBusschere Scholarship Fund

Established more than a decade ago in honor of the late Dave DeBusschere — NBA Champion, All-Star, and NBRPA Founder — this fund provides financial support to eligible NBRPA members, their spouses, children, and grandchildren pursuing degrees in higher education. The NBRPA has awarded members and their families more than $1.5 million in educational funds through this cornerstone initiative.

View on legendsofbasketball.com →
Named Scholarship
Earl Lloyd Scholarship Fund

Available to the children and grandchildren of NBRPA members who are eligible to receive assistance from the Fund. Supported by an annual gift from the NBA Players Legacy Fund, which provides significant financial support to low-income recipients.

View on legendsofbasketball.com →
HBCU Support
Legends HBCU Scholarship Program

A $10,000 scholarship, established through the NBRPA's Legends Care program, honoring the history of Historically Black Colleges and Universities while supporting current HBCU undergraduates. Recipients also gain access to the NBRPA job board, internship opportunities, complimentary NBA All-Star Weekend attendance, and post-graduation mentoring.

View on legendsofbasketball.com →
Member Grant
Member Grant Program

Direct, need-based support for individual Legends members — covering mortgage relief, medical and personal care costs, past-due debt, funeral expenses, and career development or vocational training. Maximum award is $2,500 per application; limited to one grant every two years.

View on legendsofbasketball.com →
Business Development
NBRPA Business Development Grant

Part of the NBRPA's broader player funding services — supporting members' education, health, finance, and career-change needs alongside the named scholarship funds. Contact the Scholarships & Grants subcommittee for current details.

View on legendsofbasketball.com →

To apply or learn more — contact Scholarship & Grants subcommittee leads Lionel Hollins (Lihollins14@gmail.com) or George Tinsley (Gtinsley@pengeo.com). For the complete and most current list of all scholarships and grants available through the association, visit legendsofbasketball.com.

Eligibility & Requirements

Scholarship Eligibility

Eligibility is program-specific. The following reflects the current, published eligibility rules for each fund from legendsofbasketball.com/grants-and-scholarships and legendsofbasketball.com/alumnis/membership. For program-specific questions, contact the Scholarships & Grants subcommittee leads.

🎓
Dave DeBusschere & Earl Lloyd Funds
  • DeBusschere Fund: open to eligible NBRPA members, spouses, children, and grandchildren pursuing higher-education degrees
  • Earl Lloyd Fund: open to children and grandchildren of NBRPA members, funded via the NBA Players Legacy Fund for low-income recipients
🏫
HBCU Scholarship
  • Rising full-time sophomore, junior, or senior at a four-year HBCU
  • U.S. citizen or permanent resident; minimum 3.7 GPA
  • Household income of $100,000/year or less
  • Not available to family of current/former NBA, WNBA, ABA, or Globetrotters players, or NBRPA staff/board
  • One scholarship per undergraduate career; requires tax/FAFSA docs, one faculty letter, transcripts
💵
Member Grant Program
  • Must be a Legends of Basketball member; adjusted gross income $150,000 or less
  • Cannot have received the DeBusschere or Earl Lloyd scholarship in the same or preceding year
  • Limited to one grant every two years; subject to financial/net-worth review
  • Applications due the 20th of each month; ~30 days for a decision
👤
General Membership Eligibility
  • NBA, ABA, WNBA, or Harlem Globetrotters alumni are eligible for NBRPA membership
  • Gold ($250/yr) and Platinum ($3,500 lifetime) tiers; Platinum includes scholarship fund applications
  • Health insurance access requires 3+ years of NBA service time
  • Long-tenured annual members receive credits (10%–50% off) toward Platinum upgrade
📋
How to Apply
  • Member Grant Program: mail (Attn: Director of Membership Services, 444 N. Michigan Ave., Suite 2970, Chicago, IL 60611) or email memberalert@legendsofbasketball.com
  • HBCU Scholarship: submit tax/FAFSA docs, one faculty recommendation, and transcripts
  • Named scholarship funds (DeBusschere, Earl Lloyd): contact the subcommittee leads for the current application cycle
⚠️
Important Notes
  • Member Grant Program: max $2,500/application, capped at $75,000 total annually across all recipients
  • Grant recipients must complete programmatic and financial reporting on fund usage, or become ineligible for future funding
  • Visit legendsofbasketball.com for the complete and most current eligibility requirements for each program

Questions about eligibility? Contact subcommittee leads Lionel Hollins (Lihollins14@gmail.com) or George Tinsley (Gtinsley@pengeo.com) for program-specific eligibility details.

Member Grant

Member Grant Program

Need-based financial assistance for Legends of Basketball members facing hardship or pursuing personal development opportunities.

← Back to Scholarships & Grants

About the Program

The Member Grant Program is a charitable fund with a mission to benefit, support, and assist individual Legends of Basketball members in financial need — whether facing immediate hardship or seeking support for personal development.

Eligible Funding Categories

  • Mortgage relief
  • Medical or surgical expenses
  • Past-due debt
  • Funeral expenses
  • Career development
  • Vocational training
Award

Maximum $2,500 per grant; the Finance, Audit, and Compensation Committee has discretion to reduce amounts. Capped at $75,000 total awarded annually across all recipients.

Review Cycle

Applications must arrive by the 20th of each month for that cycle's review; allow 30 days from submission for a decision.

Eligibility

  • Must be a Legends of Basketball member
  • Adjusted gross income must not exceed $150,000
  • Cannot have received the Dave DeBusschere or Earl Lloyd scholarship in the current or preceding year
  • Limited to one grant every two years
  • Preference given to WNBA members, Harlem Globetrotters alumni, and former NBA players with fewer than three full seasons

How to Apply

Submit a written request using the official application form to:

Legends of Basketball
Attn: Director of Membership Services
444 N. Michigan Ave., Suite 2970
Chicago, IL 60611

Or electronically to memberalert@legendsofbasketball.com.

Dave DeBusschere Scholarship Fund

Financial assistance for Legends of Basketball members and their families pursuing higher education, honoring the late Dave DeBusschere — NBA Champion, All-Star, and organization founder.

← Back to Scholarships & Grants

About the Fund

Established more than a decade ago in honor of the late Dave DeBusschere, this fund provides financial assistance to Legends of Basketball members and their families pursuing higher education. The fund has awarded more than $1.5 million in educational support to date.

Eligibility

  • Open to active Legends of Basketball members, their children, and their spouses
  • Grandchildren are no longer eligible
  • Dependent applicants require the member to have held active status for a minimum of two consecutive years
  • Members aged 70 or older with dependent applicants must maintain Gold Membership status ($250 dues)
  • Applicants must enroll full-time at an accredited institution — minimum 12 credit hours for undergraduates, 9 for graduate students
  • Children of deceased members have a two-year window to apply as first-time applicants
  • Previously awarded recipients of deceased members may continue receiving funds through degree completion if they maintain a 2.75+ GPA and full-time enrollment
Award

Maximum of two scholarships per member, regardless of dependent count.

How to Apply

Applications open electronically through ScholarshipAmerica during the designated window.

2026–2027 Application Timeline

  • May 4, 2026 — Applications distributed
  • June 10, 2026 — Deadline, 3:00 PM CST
  • June 29, 2026 — Determinations made
  • July 13, 2026 — Recipients announced

Questions

Direct inquiries to John Boddie at marcoms@legendsofbasketball.com, or to Scholarships & Grants subcommittee leads Lionel Hollins (Lihollins14@gmail.com) or George Tinsley (Gtinsley@pengeo.com).
